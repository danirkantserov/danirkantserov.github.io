How it works

  1. Free test

    Send one figure from a report you've already filed (a public one); I'll redraw it in your format within 2 business days.

  2. Field data

    GNSS exports with data-form IDs and the survey boundary; please remove landowner names.

  3. Deliverables

    Vector PDFs in your title block, plus GIS/CAD files.

Price
From $150 per figure set, with a fixed delivery date. First job free if unused (tell me within 30 days of delivery).
Corrections
One correction round for mapping issues, within 3 business days of your request or by a date we agree if you ask in one of my deadline weeks.
Scope
I don't do fieldwork, land surveying or wetland determinations. Non-farm sites for now. Liability limited to the fee you paid.
Your data
Confidential, used only for your job, deleted on request. AI-assisted pipeline; I check every sheet and your scientist signs. Files go through a US company's cloud AI service (training off). Rules on AI, outside processors or non-US work in your contract? Tell me first. CNDDB data: only under your license, never in an AI tool, deleted when the work ends.

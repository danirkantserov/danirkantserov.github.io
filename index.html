How it works

  1. Free test

    Send me one figure from a report you've already filed (or one that is public), and I'll redraw it in your format so you can compare. No charge, no obligation; I use the figure only for this test. I confirm the date when the figure arrives.

  2. You send the field data

    Your GNSS exports (SHP, GDB, CSV or KML) of flags, data points and photo points, with IDs matching your data forms, and the survey-area boundary. For impact exhibits and permit sheets, also the project design (CAD or PDF), the photos with their log and your CDFW extent. Public NAIP imagery unless you license other imagery. Please remove landowner names first.

  3. You get the figures and files

    Vector PDF figures in your format and title block: site location or vicinity, topography, soils, NWI (and PWI in Minnesota), FEMA flood, aerials and the delineation, plus impact exhibits and 401/1602 permit sheets as in the California sample. With them: shapefiles and a GeoPackage, a DXF in state plane coordinates and the ORM tables (CSV).

Price

From $150 per figure set (the figures and files for one delineation report). Each job gets a fixed quote and delivery date before work starts, based on acreage, features, sheets and deliverables.

First job: you pay only if you use the maps (in a submittal, a client deliverable or as the base for your own figures).

Corrections

One correction round is included for mapping issues: the format, labels or numbers on the maps, not the field boundaries. I make the corrections within 3 business days of your request, for requests made within 12 months of delivery.

Who does what

Your scientist makes every wetland determination, reviews every figure before submittal and signs the report. I don't do fieldwork, land surveying or wetland determinations; sites in agricultural production are out of scope for now. My liability for a job is limited to the fee you paid for it.

Confidentiality

Your data, project and client details are confidential and used only for your job. I'll sign your NDA if it allows the data handling described here.

Data handling

Your files stay on my computer and in the channel you send them through; I send deliverables through a private folder shared only with you. Some processing is AI-assisted (Anthropic's Claude, with model training turned off), so parts of your files may pass through Anthropic's service; I share them with no one else. If your contract forbids AI tools or offshore handling of the data, tell me before you send anything. Please remove landowner names and other personal details. Your data is deleted on request, otherwise at the end of the correction period.

White-label

On payment, you own the deliverables and all rights in them. They are white-label: your title block, your firm named as creator, my name only if you want it.
